My YouTube algorithm gave me a weird looking thumbnail video and I don't know what moved me, but I decided to check it out.
This was "90s" by **The Lazy Boys**.

I loved it immediately and put it in my Favourites.

After a while I tried looking up some more info, but it was very difficult to find. Partly because it's so niche, but also 
because it seems like Japanese bands are not really marketing per se in Western media?

Anyway, after a while I got this Gemini blurb:

> The Lazy Boys is a Tokyo-based Japanese music project and alternative rock/hip-hop/punk unit led by graphic artist VERDY along with close friends from the music scene. They have released singles like "One Thing" (2023) and "New Level" (2026)

At first I thought this was maybe a hallucination, even after specfically looking for "Verdy band" etcetera I could not find any real good definitive answers.

So I looked up Verdy, a name I've also never heard, and turns out he's "big in Japan", but then for real. He's a famous artist/designer and is working
with quite some big names. His influences are a little punky/skatey and this fortified the idea that he might really be behind The Lazy Boys concept.

Some spelunking later, I found more information. He's the person behind this 'collective' and every album (or maybe song?) he invites friends to play these songs with.
The names are other rock bands from Japan, most of which I haven't heard yet either.

There were so many twists in this journey that I figured I'd write about it. Oh yeah, he makes the album art himself, so sorry for calling "New Level" weird?
Anyway, check out some of [their music](https://www.tunecore.co.jp/artists/thelazyboys?lang=en)!
