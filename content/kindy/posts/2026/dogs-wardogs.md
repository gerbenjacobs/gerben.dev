As you might [have](/tags/wardogs) [seen](/posts/game-idea-zombie-town) I've been playing a bit of Wardogs lately. I've spent 155 hours in the game since September 10th.
Yes, if you do the calculations, that's a lot. I've had some.. um.. [free time](/posts/life-update-august-2026). 🤔

There's a lot of things I like about the game, it's basically the Battlefield that I remember, that I wished for since BF4.
It's a more engaging game than #Squad, but it could also use some more pointers from Squad itself.

I'll just start with a list of things I like. Because the other list will be longer, but that's only because I already care about the game and would like to see it improve.

- Money as the main currency.
- 3 teams, instead of 2.
- The gunplay is pretty decent.
- The graphics are nice and the maps are well designed.
- Proximity chat with everyone, including enemies.

And to summarize, the core of the game is fun. There's just a bunch of balancing or design issues that make it less fun than it could be.

**Mortar spam**

It's way too easy (and too cheap) to fire 3 mortar rounds in a row. The person firing also doesn't have to interact with the game in any way, they can use a mortar someone else built, they'll use the ammo someone else brought, they fire from a distance and get to see a real time video feed of the target area. It's also very quick to reload and do it again, on the receiving end you don't even have enough time to drag your wounded buddies back to cover before the next volley.

This is my biggest gripe with the game. I don't even care that much about the IR Range finder or the Havoc.

**Snipers**

Okay, this is difficult. I never really like snipers in games, it's the same kind of feeling as with mortars; snipers are out somewhere on the fringes, taking potshots at people within the zone who have to worry about a dozen angles of attack, add on top of that most marksman- and sniper rifles are heavy hitters, so getting two-tapped by someone playing a completely different game just sucks.

There's really no good solution for this.

**Late game OP gear**

This list is not in order, so don't think this is my 3rd gripe. But this fits a lot with the "two-tapped" issue from the snipers. In the late game guns like the FAL and BMR are just too good, on any reasonable armor type. In a game that doesn't have an obvious frontline, getting two-tapped from any angle is just frustrating gameplay.

**Cost of infantry kits**

We now come to the root of why things feel frustrating. Entering battle with a decent kit sets you back 15k, something that takes several minutes in the control zone, some revives and some kills, to even break even. Now imagine you run up against a level 4 armored BMR-wielding gunner after your first kill. You're down -12k. (And yes, occasionally that would be fine, but this happens 5 times every game)

There's now even a bit of a meta where you go in with a very cheap kit (like the free gun, bag and resuscitator) and just scavenge from dead players. Because that's so much more profitable.

 
> Yeah before prices of gold bars dropped I didn’t mind dropping 20-30 k kits without profit, i was breaking even and having fun each match.
>
> Since the drop in price I’ve been a loot scavenging rat and now only profiting but I feel like I’m having less fun, plus I looked at the skins the other day and realised there’s really nothing I actually care about so I’m back to buying kits and going negative.
>
> dinosaurrawrxd (on Reddit)

**Money economy**

I said I like money as the game's currency, but the economy needs tweaking. There needs to be some balancing when it comes to how easily it is to use a system to make money, versus how bad it is to lose against. A good example is the Stingray, this is a UAV missile that costs 4.5k and needs to lock on to a target for 3 seconds to arm itself. If you hit me with that in my Humvee, APC or static CWIS; fine, you win.

But if you hop on a mortar with no kit and you use a website to get proper coordinates to fire 3 rounds in a row, without any cost on your side; I'll say "go fuck yourself" in All-chat. 

A very difficult example is RPGs. For use in the Control Zone I think they're fine, maybe a bit too cheap. But it's such an occasional weapon system that hardly anyone ever brings it to the battlefield. (Which is maybe a design issue on its own). But when people get a Kodiak SUV full of rockets and start harassing logistics players behind enemy lines, then I'd hope it would cost them way more.

**Asymmetrical gameplay**

This continues from the RPG SUV example. I think it should be okay to harass the backlines, but not continuously. I think it should be okay to be an OP sniper getting headshots (cause granted, they feel nice). But when it becomes incessant, it ruins the game for the receiving party.

I like C4 jeeps from back in the Battlefield era and so far they've been fine in Wardogs too. As long as it just doesn't become an accepted meta.

This is a really difficult #gamedesign issue I assume, because you want to keep the freedom/sandboxy feeling of the game, but also put in some guardrails against 'abuse'.

**Abuse**

This is really not a good header for this, but it's a continuation from the last point and there's only one thing that fits here; Grenade Launcher Heli.

These players also get my rage in All-chat as well as getting reported under the category "Griefing". Cause this meta is so annoying, even people on their own team hate these guys.

People use a 6k heli and use its inventory as a quick reload for the passengers on the sidesteps, who keep shooting grenades from their noobtubes. There's hardly a counter, because these helipilots are often skilled and keep circle strafing. There's no breathing room, because there's often 2 or 3 GLs and because of the 'bug' they have no downtime for reloading. Full on abuse, and I hope these people will get restricted (bans should probably be reserved for actual cheaters).

As a side note; I also hate the players who say “if it’s in the game, it’s allowed”. 🙄

**Lack of teamplay**

This is a big topic. It starts with the assumption that the devs made that by incentivising teamplay with money, it will happen more; and this is true. But to an extent, on one side there’s people who go for ‘rambo revives’ (not sure if this term from back in the day is still used), they try to revive you as quick as possible because it’s good money. But sometimes this is without smoke, with the enemy that shot you still alive, you being in a horrible place when you died, or sometimes all the above. On the other side, the literal opposite, people are too afraid to get you and just leave you, while as a bigger group you actually stand to last longer. 

> I'm with you on that one. There should be room to maneuver without having to fight an enemy for every house you pass. I think the problem lies more in the matches where half your team is lying dead with a 20k kit hoping for a revive, while the other half is busy fobbing or doing logistics, leaving one man on a solo mission trying to run over to rez a couple of the boys only to be killed by the same sniper who got the last 3 trying to do the same thing.
> 
> Kittelsen (on Reddit)

I play a lot on North America (East) servers because, just as with Squad, US servers have a lot more communication (sometimes too much) but it’s way better than EU servers where nobody dares to speak because English is not their native language (I’m assuming here). Bonus points that, in my opinion, there are more noob-players in US servers. At 90 ping I sometimes have even better ping than Americans from the West or South.

I’ve played with a Brit before who was 'very extroverted' (at least, that’s how it appeared to me) but he was very nice and kind, he was literally leading a band of us through the hotzone and teamplay shined. Looking back it’s really as simple as “hey xx-name, you’re down, I’m coming to res you” and “Circle is moving, let’s move up boys”. Just keeping motivation high.

The last bastion of teamplay that I see right now is that FOB builders and logi drivers/pilots talk in chat about what resources they want. Occasionally there’s a squad that sticks together in the hotzone and has a good time, but  they’re few and far between. Also once you lose critical mass it’s difficult to survive, and afterwards also very difficult to get the gang back together, unless you literally communicate it and wait to take a heli ride together.

**Green 🙊, red 🙉, blue 🙈**

The memes are true. And at first (beta and first 2 weeks) red was known as the Hotzone chasers, but now they're just "the second team".

Green is successfully chasing the hot zone, reviving each other and leaving nobody behind. Meanwhile they still have a dedicated group of FOB builders who can call in the drill; although almost never need to.

Red is trying to engage in the hotzone, has 1 (or sometimes 2 competing) FOBs, but also a group that goes after Blue because they're 'easy kills'.

Blue is blue. Anyone who doesn't understand the meta, doesn't care that much about winning, or is just trying to do their own thing. Blue always has the most FOBs, they never seem to be able to keep it. I'm glad as a heli pilot I get paid when I drop my pallets, because half of it never gets used.

I think it has been discussed also in the community, that Blue's camo and position on the map is _scientifically_ worse than the other factions. So all these other problems just compound.

I sometimes join Blue to check, and I had a good piloting round the other day, but each time I'm just; fuck nah. I gave up on red when they stopped chasing HZ.

I really like the Green playstyle, and if I'm tired of that, I can easily do piloting or just logi runs with Urals. But I've also noticed when we steamroll too hard, it's not fun either. A bit of challenge is really required. It's also impossible to run out of defibrillator batteries on Green, cause everyone has them and they're all rushing to get the revive.

**FOBs**

This was not actually on my list, and don't get me wrong, I really like the idea of FOBs and building and construction and the logi runs that it requires. But..

Within the entire gameplay loop (or 'the meta' if you will) they really don't add anything. Well maybe the anti-air assets do..

Again, I don't have a solution for this, but together with the Towers that they're often around, this part of the game could use some ✨️gamedesign✨️.
