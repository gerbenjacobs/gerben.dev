I initially saw a vocal [cover for Alesana - Apology](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DfIUFVizKqU/), one of the best songs in the world.

After some snooping I found their band instragram; [**@fawnspeak**](https://www.instagram.com/fawnspeak/). They have just released their first EP titled **Lilith** in August, based on the actual religious/folkore story of [Lilith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilith).

<figure class="center"><img src="/kd/images/blog/2026/fawnspeak-lilith.jpg" alt="Album cover for Lilith, showing a woman with dark long hair, covering her face with both hands while holding a rosary bead with a cross on it." class="u-photo"></figure>

>  This concept ep being our first body of work was a huge undertaking for us. Lilith is in no way a religious album, but is us sharing the story of a woman scorned by the male figures in her life. Through sharing the folklore of Lilith, we aim to shed a light on the power women behold and the feminist icon Lilith is. Each track walks you through her life and what ultimately ended up turning her into a demon, monster of the night.

I just love Kiana's screams and then when the cleans come in, it's just perfection. 👨‍🍳👌   
On its own it's good, but knowing it's a two-person endeavour, their first release, the mixing sounds really good, it's just a complete package.

Check out the song **"Her Side"** by **Fawn Speak**.
