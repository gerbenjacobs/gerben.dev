Took an NS train from Schiphol Airport Station to Brussels Midi (which is their south station). 
Unfortunately something happend on the HSL-line (high speed) that caused us to sit idle in Rotterdam for a while,
before we were let go, with the caveat that we had to take a detour, that eventually would take 40 minutes extra.

Luckily we had nothing planned, so after having some drinks when we arrived, we went for dinner at an Italian place.
We arrived late for my north-European dinner time schedule, but my bourgondic Italian and French colleagues were used to it. 
I had a lovely pasta dish with garlic and spicy oil, by the way.

It was the first time meeting my Belgian colleagues face to face, as well as my first time in Brussels to begin with.
Our manager had booked a room for half the day, but as you know in corporate land, it's very difficult to find a meeting room,
so we had to take one in the basement, the least decorated floor of the building.

However the building was initially owned by an actual bank and so there was this massive vault door. So that's kinda cool!

<figure class="center">
  <img src="/kd/images/blog/2026/brussel/PXL_20261104_101639828.jpg" alt="Bank vault door in the basement" class="u-photo">
</figure>

Our hotel was quite posh, there were a lot of people in suits or just very nicely dressed. It's relatively close to the European Commission building, as well as other embassies. We even saw the bus for Red Bull FC Salzburg. They'll be playing Anderlecht tomorrow. The breakfast was good, but the sound isolation was quite poor, would personally not pay this much for a room.

Bruxelles itself is very nice, their metro is kinda old, but functioning. It's a big city that feels small, kinda like Amsterdam.
We also rode past Mechelen which reminded me a lot of Arnhem, including even their newly created stations architecture.
